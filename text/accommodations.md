accommodations

The conference has a block of rooms at the [Hyatt Place](https://www.hyatt.com/hyatt-place/en-US/smfzu-hyatt-place-uc-davis/rooms) on the UC Davis campus, a comfortable hotel located conveniently near the conference center and, for walking, running, or simply perusal, the [UC Davis Arboretum.](https://arboretum.ucdavis.edu/sites/g/files/dgvnsk1546/files/inline-files/visitor_map_2025%20%281%29_0.pdf) The Arboretum is also a pleasant route to walk to and from downtown.

![The redwood grove in the UC Davis Arboretum](./images/redwood.png){fig-alt="The redwood grove in the UC Davis Arboretum" .lightbox}

Should you wish to make alternative arrangements you might try the [Aggie Inn,](https://www.choicehotels.com/california/davis/ascend-hotels/cad04) the [Best Western Plus Palm Court Davis,](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/book/hotels-in-davis/best-western-plus-palm-court-hotel/propertyCode.05536.html) or the [Hilton Garden Inn Davis Downtown,](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/smfdagi-hilton-garden-inn-davis-downtown/) all in town and quite close to campus, and more or less along a route from the train station to the conference center.
